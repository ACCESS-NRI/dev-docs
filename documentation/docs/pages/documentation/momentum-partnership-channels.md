This page documents the communication channels used by ACCESS-NRI and partner organizations, such as the Momentum Partnership.

## Momentum Partnership communication channels

URLs:
* [MOSRS](https://code.metoffice.gov.uk/) (MOSRS login required).
* [MOSRS Yammer Feed](https://www.yammer.com/metoffice.gov.uk/#/threads/inGroup?type=in_group&feedId=10997884) (UK Met Office Microsoft 365 login required).
* [Momentum Partnership Sharepoint](https://metoffice.sharepoint.com/sites/MomentumPartnership/SitePages/Home.aspx) (Microsoft 365 login required).
* [Momentum Partnership Teams](https://teams.microsoft.com/v2/?tenantId=17f18161-20d7-4746-87fd-50fe3e3b6619) (Microsoft 365 login required).
* [Simulation Systems Working Practices](https://metoffice.github.io/simulation-systems/) (Open to read).
* [Simulation Systems Discussion](https://github.com/MetOffice/simulation-systems/discussions) (Open to read, GitHub login required for write access).
* [GitHub MetOffice Repositories](https://github.com/orgs/MetOffice/repositories?type=all&q=sort%3Aname-asc&page=1) (Open to Read).
  * Includes [FAB](https://github.com/MetOffice/fab).
* [GitHub Met Office Modelling Infrastructure Support Systems Repositories](https://github.com/metomi) (Open to Read).
  * Includes [FCM](https://github.com/metomi/fcm) and [Rose](https://github.com/metomi/rose).
  * [FCM Documentation](https://metomi.github.io/fcm/doc/) (Open to Read).
  * [Rose Documentation](https://metomi.github.io/rose/2019.01.8/html/index.html) (Open to Read).
* [Cylc Repositories](https://github.com/cylc?q=&type=all&language=&sort=name) (Open to Read).
  * Includes [Cylc](https://github.com/cylc/cylc-flow).
  * [Cylc Documentation](https://cylc.github.io/cylc-doc/7.9.3/html/index.html) (Open to Read).
