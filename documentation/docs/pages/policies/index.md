Policies are the rules for software development at ACCESS-NRI and for its GitHub organisation. Unlike [guidelines](../guidelines/index.md), they are mandatory.

Each policy page shows its current status. How policies are approved and changed is being discussed in [dev-docs#38](https://github.com/ACCESS-NRI/dev-docs/issues/38).
